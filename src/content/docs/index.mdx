These docs are the engineering companion to the [Infopole](https://infopole.org)
**Denied Party Screening (DPS)** platform — written for developers and technical
reviewers. They favour *why* over *what*: the decisions, trade-offs, and
failure-modes behind a real, multi-tenant compliance system and its AI layer.
